ShopPilot — AI-Powered IT Shop Assistant

ShopPilot is an AI-powered digital assistant designed for physical IT product shops that sell laptops, desktop PCs, and accessories. The goal is to help businesses automate product inquiries, support customers, and improve digital marketing through an intelligent chatbot and AI agent.

*** Project Goals

Provide an AI-powered chatbot for customer inquiries.

Display IT product information, specifications, prices, and stock availability.

Help customers find suitable products based on their requirements.

Automate digital marketing tasks and promotional content generation.

Build a foundation for future business workflow automation.

*** Planned Features

Initial project setup

Load product catalogs from CSV files

Search products by name and specifications

Answer product and pricing questions

Integrate an LLM for conversational responses

Develop a customer-facing chatbot interface

Generate marketing content for social media

Add inventory-aware responses

Integrate business workflows
